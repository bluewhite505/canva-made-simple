What exactly is included in the vault?

The vault contains ready-made planner, journal, tracker and other product designs totaling 7,500 editable pages. They are product designs to customize, not 7,500 individual finished products. Access is through a PDF directory of Canva links.

You can learn core Canva tools with a free account. Individual templates or elements may require Pro access or paid licenses. Check each design before using or exporting it.
