Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026 · This notice explains data processing for the Canva Made Simple storefront.

This Privacy Policy explains how Lila Vey (trading as pinprofitgirl) handles information relating to visits and purchases of Canva Made Simple, Ready-Made Digital Product Vault and the Digital Product Starter Bundle.

The data controller responsible for the storefront is Lila Vey (pinprofitgirl). For privacy questions and requests, contact fociva.tech@gmail.com.

2. What information may be processed

  • Checkout details: email address, billing/customer details, payment status, purchase items and transaction identifiers submitted during Stripe checkout.

We process necessary order and delivery data to perform our contract with you; payment, accounting and tax records to comply with applicable legal obligations; and limited security, fraud-prevention and operational information where we have a legitimate interest and that basis is available. We use Stripe to process checkout and payments; Cloudflare for backend verification, downloads and file storage; and GitHub Pages to host the public website. Those companies may process technical or transactional information according to their roles, contracts and privacy notices. We do not sell customer data to advertisers.

5. Cookies and third-party pages

The public landing-page code does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. Hosting providers may process technical information, while Stripe checkout and linked third-party pages may use cookies or similar technologies subject to their policies. Visiting an external Canva template link is governed by Canva's own privacy terms.

6. Retention and security

We keep data only for as long as reasonably necessary for transactions, delivery, lawful recordkeeping, support and dispute handling, subject to local statutory retention requirements. The products are intended for adults and are not marketed to children. We may update this notice when practices or legal requirements change. The date at the top identifies the version shown here.
